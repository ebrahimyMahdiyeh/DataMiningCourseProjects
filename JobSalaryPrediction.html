        continue

🗣 To ensure fair training and avoid bias toward the majority class, we apply oversampling to balance the dataset. In our case, the number of Dissatisfied employees is significantly lower than Satisfied, which can cause the model to favor the dominant class during learning. We use Random Oversampling to increase the number of samples in the minority class by duplicating them until both classes are equally represented. This helps the model learn to identify patterns from both classes more accurately and prevents misleadingly high accuracy due to imbalance.
